Every CSV in this folder is fictional sample data for Helio Grill, a made-up venue.

foot_traffic.csv is shaped like an hourly people-count a computer-vision counter on existing CCTV would write out (one row per day and hour, entries = people counted). This repository does not read video, connect to cameras, or run a detector.

Staff names, availability, skills, and pay rates are invented. Pay rates are base rates, not award rates.
